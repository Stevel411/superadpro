How incoming sales work

Getting paid, and approving a sale.

Buyers pay you directly, wallet to wallet — AdvantageLife never holds your money. Here's the flow, and exactly where each sale goes.

1

A buyer picks a pack

Someone buys a campaign pack through your link. The sale is matched to you — or passed up your chain, depending on where you are in your cycle.

2

They pay you directly

Wallet to wallet, straight to your receiving method. The platform never touches the funds — it just records who to pay and the proof.

3

The sale lands in your queue

It appears under “Incoming sales” with a notification. You'll see who it's from and how much.

4

Check your wallet first

Confirm the money has actually arrived before you approve. Only approve once you've genuinely been paid.

5

Tap confirm

Confirming activates the buyer's pack and advances your sale counter. It's final — it can't be undone.
