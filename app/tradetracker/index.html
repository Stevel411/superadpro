Trading Strategy

📖 Three Timeframe Trading System

📊 Overview

Trading Style: Intraday Position Trading (15M execution)

Target: 3-4 quality trades per day

Primary Pairs: GBP/USD, EUR/USD, USD/JPY

Sessions: London (3:00 AM - 12:00 PM EST) & New York (8:00 AM - 5:00 PM EST)

📅 Daily Chart - "The Boss"

Purpose: Determine overall bias

  • Identify trend direction
  • Mark S/R zones
  • Decide trade direction

⚠️ DO NOT TRADE off Daily - Analysis only!

⏱️ One-Hour Chart - "The Manager"

Purpose: Identify key levels

  • Mark POC, VAH, VAL
  • Mark liquidity pools
  • Set alerts at key levels
  • Watch for price interaction

⚡ Fifteen-Minute Chart - "The Executor"

Purpose: Execute ALL trades

  • Watch price at 1H levels
  • Wait for breakout + confirmation
  • Enter on retest
  • Manage position on 15M

✅ ALL ENTRIES ON 15M ONLY!

🕐 Trading Sessions

London Session (3:00 AM - 12:00 PM EST)

  • High volatility at open (3:00 AM - 6:00 AM EST)
  • Best for GBP/USD and EUR/USD
  • Major economic news releases
  • Strong directional moves

New York Session (8:00 AM - 5:00 PM EST)

  • High volatility at open (8:00 AM - 11:00 AM EST)
  • London-NY overlap (8:00 AM - 12:00 PM EST) = highest liquidity
  • USD pairs most active
  • US economic data releases

💡 Best Trading: London-NY overlap (8:00 AM - 12:00 PM EST)

⚠️ Risk Management Rules

  • 1% max risk per trade
  • 4 trades max per day
  • 2 consecutive losses = mandatory break
  • Never move stop away from entry
  • Use lot size calculator before every trade
  • Avoid trading during low liquidity periods
  • No trading 30 minutes before/after major news

✅ Pre-Market Checklist

Review Daily bias on Daily chart
Mark key levels on 1H chart (POC, VAH, VAL)
Set alerts at key levels
Calculate position size for planned trades
Confirm trading session is active (London/NY)
Check economic calendar for high-impact news
Review yesterday's trades and journal notes
Confirm max 4 trades rule for today

📋 Trading Rules Reminder

  • Trade ONLY during London session (3AM - 12PM EST)
  • Maximum 4 trades per day
  • Risk ONLY 1% per trade
  • Take mandatory break after 2 consecutive losses
  • Execute ALL trades on 15-minute chart
  • Never move stop loss away from entry
  • Always use lot size calculator before entering
  • Wait for confirmation before entering position
