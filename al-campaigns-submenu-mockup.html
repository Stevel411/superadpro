“My Campaigns” becomes a menu

Tapping My Campaigns expands a little sub-menu instead of just opening one page — so a brand-new member (like aron) sees Create Campaign right there, no hunting through a tab row.

Proposed sub-items:

  • View campaigns → the list of their running ads (/campaigns)
  • Create Campaign → the create form (/create-campaign)
  • Performance → their campaign stats (/campaign-analytics)

The parent still opens the list on tap; the chevron expands the menu. Active sub-item gets the red highlight. Same navy/red AL styling as the rest of the sidebar.

Happy with this, or want it trimmed to just Create Campaign under it?
